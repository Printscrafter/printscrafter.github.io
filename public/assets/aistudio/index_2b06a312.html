Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Printscrafter

Where is Printscrafter located and do you deliver across Nigeria and internationally?

Printscrafter is located in Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Nigeria. We offer nationwide delivery within 3–5 business days across all states in Nigeria (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, etc.) and express global shipping within 7–10 business days.

Can I upload my own custom photo or personal image to render in the 3D Room Visualizer?

Yes! You can click the "Upload Local Image to 3D Wall" button inside our 3D Visualizer Studio to instantly preview your personal family photo, digital artwork, or custom photography on virtual walls in Living Room or Dining Room setups.

How do Illuminated Smart LED Canvases work?

Our Smart LED Canvas collection features low-voltage ambient backlighting with integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth app controls. They support dynamic music synchronization, voice controls, and customizable color modes.

What framing options and materials do you offer?

We craft gallery-wrapped archival cotton canvas prints, HD aluminum metal panels, 3D plaster reliefs, and offer frame finishes in Modern Black, Natural Oak Wood, Brushed Gold Metal, or Frameless Canvas Wraps.
